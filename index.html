🕹️ How to Play

  1. 1️⃣ A blurred image appears — your job is to figure out what it shows.
  2. 2️⃣ The blur melts continuously. Faster guesses score more points.
  3. 3️⃣ Correct guess → Round ends instantly. Score = time remaining × 10 (max 300!).
  4. 4️⃣ Wrong guess → 5-second lockout and −50 points. The image keeps coming into focus and the clock keeps ticking while you're locked out — the correct answer stays hidden until you tap it or time runs out!
  5. 5️⃣ If the timer hits zero, the round ends with 0 points and the correct answer and image are fully revealed.
